Renamed the first-launch "Terms & Conditions" to "Legal Disclaimer" and moved the safety notice out of it into its own popup that appears right after you accept, with an OK button and a "Don't Show Again" option. The safety notice is also available from Help & Info.
Fixed the route line from your position to the next waypoint staying stuck on the first waypoint as you advanced along the route. It now follows your current target, and is drawn in the same green as the rest of the route, a bit more prominent.
The active route line (from your position to the next waypoint) is now solid, and all the dashed legs between waypoints are the same width as it. The dashed leg between the previous and next waypoint now stays visible underneath the solid approach line instead of disappearing once you've passed a waypoint.
The wind direction arrow on the weather widget now has a clearer wind icon on it, so it's more obvious that it's showing wind direction.
When weather info isn't available yet, the weather widget now shows a clear "Weather unavailable" state instead of an empty placeholder, and retries every 15 seconds until it gets data.
The temperature trend on the weather widget now shows the upcoming change over the next hour (forecast vs. now) instead of the change over the past hour.
Added Android Auto support: project weather, your route, points of interest, search, and settings to your car's screen as a non-navigation app.
The Android Auto screen now shows your live map on the car display — base map, weather radar, your route line, and your location — with pan and pinch-to-zoom and automatic day/night styling.
On Android Auto, weather now appears as a compact card on the map (temperature, trend, wind direction arrow, and wind speed) instead of a text list — matching the phone, and freezing to a static reading while driving.
Weather card: removed the wind icon, added a 'Wind' label below the arrow, and slightly reduced the arrow size for a cleaner look.
Route line on Android Auto now matches the phone (bright green), and waypoint circles + numbers now render on the car map. Fixed a regression on the phone where the dashed planned-route segments between waypoints were missing.
